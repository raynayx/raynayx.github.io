Breathe
Dance this dance with me 
And yet take no steps 
Sing this song with me
And yet say no words

Come
Sit here with me
Let us stare into the abyss of knowledge 
So abundant that wisdom is scarce
Someday, we may find a piece

Watch
The new leaf open its palm
Like a child learning to accept a nut
See the vinyebu burn until its embers die out

Sit
Watch hundreds of bleyivi in the beach sand
Dance to the war drums of the aʋadada
Wielding their pincers like spears
Someday,
You and I will chase them and catch only one

One day
We will no longer be here --
Neither you nor I

For today, come 
For now,  sit
This moment, just breathe 
And do nothing with me
